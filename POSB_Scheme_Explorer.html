Sources & method

How the numbers are worked out.

Scheme rates are those notified by the Department of Economic Affairs, Ministry of Finance, for 1 October – 31 December 2026 (unchanged from July – September 2026). Bank rates are representative published retail deposit rates checked against official bank pages available in October 2026; where a bank does not publish a directly comparable product/tenor, the table explicitly labels a proxy.

How each scheme is estimated

  • TD: compounded quarterly. NSC: compounded yearly, paid at maturity. KVP: doubles in 115 months at the current rate.
  • RD: deposits at the start of each month, interest on each month's balance, added to the balance every quarter.
  • MIS / SCSS: interest is paid out monthly / quarterly and not reinvested; principal returns at maturity.
  • PPF / SSY: yearly deposits, interest on the lowest monthly balance and credited at year end. SSY deposits run for 15 years and the account matures at 21 years. Both rates are reviewed every quarter, so future years may differ.
  • After tax: interest is taxed at your slab every year and the rest is reinvested. PPF and SSY interest is tax-free. Surcharge, cess, TDS and deduction limits are ignored.
  • Effective yield: the single yearly rate that would turn your deposits into the final amount, so schemes with different payment patterns can be compared.
These are planning estimates. Actual maturity amounts follow scheme rules, rounding and deposit dates. Do not quote them as official maturity values.
