Statutory Time Limits & Claim Settlement Clocks

Government of India and Postal Directorate mandatory submission deadlines, claim windows, and administrative penalty timelines.

Interactive Deadline Calculator

Leave Travel Concession (LTC) Adjustment 30 / 60 Days

If advance taken: submit within 30 days. If no advance drawn: submit within 60 days. Default leads to recovery of advance with penal interest.

Inspection Report (IR) Objection Compliance 14 Days

Sub Postmasters must respond to Inspection Objections by SDI(P) / ASP within 14 days of receiving the inspection note.

Speed Post Loss Compensation Claim 30 Days

Customer complaint for loss or delay must be filed within 30 days from the date of booking of the Speed Post article.

Deceased Claim Settlement Window 7 Working Days

Nominee registered claims must be settled within 1 working day; Non-nominee claims without legal evidence within 7 working days upon completion of verification.
